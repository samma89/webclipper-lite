# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-29_

Clipper Lite is built so that your data never leaves your device.

## What the extension does with your data

- **Clips stay local.** When you save a clip, its content (HTML, title, URL, and basic page metadata such as author and description) is stored in your browser's own IndexedDB storage for this extension.
- **No servers.** The extension has no backend and makes no network requests of its own. Nothing is uploaded, synced, or shared.
- **No accounts.** There is nothing to sign in to.
- **No analytics or tracking.** The extension collects no usage data, telemetry, crash reports, or identifiers.
- **Pages are read only on request.** The extension reads a page only after you click its toolbar button, use its keyboard shortcut, or choose one of its right-click menu items.

## Things to be aware of

- A clip can include images and stylesheets that the page loads from other sites. When you view a clip in the library, your browser may fetch those images from their original servers, just as it would when you visit the page.
- Exports (Markdown, HTML, JSON) are files you download yourself. What you do with them is up to you.
- Removing the extension, or clearing its data in Chrome, permanently deletes your library. Export first if you want a backup.

## Permissions

The extension requests `activeTab`, `scripting`, `contextMenus`, and `unlimitedStorage`. Each is used only for the functionality described above and explained in the [README](README.md#permissions).
